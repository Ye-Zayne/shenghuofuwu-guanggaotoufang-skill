## Hook Selection

- Price hook: use when offer is strong and compliant.
- Pain hook: use when the service solves an urgent or embarrassing problem.
- Result hook: use when before/after is visually strong.
- Local hook: use when商圈、距离、节假日、周末场景 matters.
- Trust hook: use when category needs expertise or safety.
- Scarcity hook: use when there is real limited stock, slots, or dates.
